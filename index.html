Our Mission

A person’s past should not permanently determine their future.

Shafanna Solutions is a proposed Detroit-based, Black-founded nonprofit dedicated to helping individuals and families overcome barriers to stability, employment, health, housing, education, and long-term economic independence.

Through coordinated wraparound services, workforce development, housing assistance, healthcare navigation, recovery support, food security, education, and community partnerships, Shafanna Solutions seeks to create practical pathways from hardship to self-sufficiency.

Rooted in Detroit and committed to equitable access, Shafanna Solutions seeks to build a community in which every person has the opportunity, resources, and support to finish stronger than they started.

Who we are focused on serving

Our commitment is especially focused on people facing significant barriers — not as categories, but as neighbors rebuilding on their own terms.

  • Returning citizens coming home and rebuilding
  • People experiencing homelessness or housing instability
  • Individuals affected by substance use disorders
  • Unemployed and underemployed adults
  • Families navigating economic instability
  • Entrepreneurs and aspiring small business owners
  • First-time homebuyers preparing to purchase a home

One-door promise: Someone seeking employment can also receive help with housing, transportation, treatment referrals, food, clothing, and training — without restarting the process with every department.

Get Help

One entry point. One plan. One team walking with you.

This is how the Shafanna Solutions model will work when programs launch. There is no intake form on this public site and no personal information is collected here — this section describes the process so you know what to expect.

01

Community Intake & Navigation

We start by listening and assessing needs together — housing, work, health, food, family, and legal referrals — in a single conversation, not five separate applications.

02

Your navigator & support plan

You are assigned a navigator who helps build an individualized support plan, coordinates services, and stays as your single point of contact as steps change.

03

A pathway forward

Services are coordinated around your plan — so progress in one area (for example, training) is supported in the others (housing, food, clothing, and health access).

Status note: Shafanna Solutions is currently in formation. Intake, appointments, and referrals will open through a separate, secure system once formation and partnerships are complete. This public site does not collect health, housing, or other sensitive information.

Reentry Services

Coming home deserves a plan — and a person in your corner from day one.

Reentry is its own dedicated service at Shafanna Solutions, not an afterthought. Returning citizens will be paired with a navigator from day one who helps coordinate the practical steps of rebuilding — so housing, work, benefits, and family support move together, not in isolation.

  • ID and documents navigation — help obtaining identification, vital records, and the paperwork needed for housing, work, and benefits
  • Housing pathways — emergency, transitional, and supportive housing navigation for returning citizens
  • Employment pathways — job placement, career coaching, work clothing, and employer partnerships that welcome returning citizens
  • Benefits enrollment — Medicaid and benefits navigation, food assistance, and recovery support connections
  • Record-related legal referrals — connection to qualified legal support through Justice & Advocacy
  • A navigator from day one — one point of contact who knows your plan and walks with you as it changes

Reentry support connects directly to Housing & Reentry, Employment, Health & Recovery, and Family Support — because coming home touches all of them at once.

Entrepreneurship & Small Business

Build the business — with classes, coaching, and a plan lenders can read.

Shafanna Solutions helps entrepreneurs turn an idea or an early-stage business into something sustainable. Support is practical and step-by-step, whether you are starting from scratch, formalizing work you already do, or preparing to grow.

  • Business Classes — practical classes hosted through Shafanna Academy, covering starting, running, and growing a small business
  • Startup coaching — choosing a structure, registering, and setting up the basics
  • Business-planning coaching — writing and refining a plan you can actually use
  • Help preparing to work with lenders and small-business programs — organizing financials, documents, and next steps before you apply

Hosted by Shafanna Academy: Business Classes are part of the Academy calendar, so entrepreneurs learn alongside the same wraparound support — coaching, food, and navigation stay connected while you build.

Homebuyer Education

Home buyer classes — so you buy ready, not rushed.

Buying a first home is a milestone and a process. Shafanna Solutions will offer home buyer classes and one-on-one navigation to help Detroit residents prepare with confidence — from first budget to closing readiness.

  • Home buyer classes — a clear walk-through of the homebuying process, step by step
  • Homebuyer readiness — knowing when you are ready and what to do next
  • Budgeting and credit preparation — building the financial foundation lenders look for
  • Understanding mortgages — terms, costs, and questions to ask before you sign
  • Navigation to down-payment assistance programs — help finding and applying to programs you may qualify for

Shafanna Solutions provides education and navigation/referrals for homebuyers. Shafanna Solutions is not HUD-certified and is not MSHDA-approved at this time; where a certified program is required, your navigator will connect you to an approved provider or program.

Health & Recovery

Navigation and referrals — clearly and honestly.

Shafanna Solutions plans to help people enroll in and navigate Michigan Medicaid and other benefits, and connect them to independent, licensed behavioral-health and treatment providers.

Shafanna Solutions does not provide clinical care and does not bill Medicaid. Any clinical care would be provided by independent, licensed healthcare providers through future referral arrangements — not by Shafanna Solutions; Shafanna Solutions’ planned role is to help you access care, understand coverage, and stay connected as part of your overall plan.

Get Help Explore Services

What this includes

  • Medicaid navigation — enrollment help, renewals, and understanding coverage
  • Benefits navigation alongside housing, food, and family support
  • Behavioral-health referrals to licensed providers
  • Substance-use treatment referrals and recovery support connections
  • Follow-up through your navigator, so a referral doesn’t become a dead end

Community-based support in Detroit — food distribution as one part of a coordinated pathway, not a standalone stop.

Shafanna Academy

Credentials that open doors, on a schedule that respects real life.

Shafanna Academy is the planned education arm of Shafanna Solutions: GED pathways, workforce certifications, and original classes designed around employer and community needs — including Business Classes for entrepreneurs and home buyer classes for first-time buyers.

Academy learning is paired with the wraparound model — coaching, essentials, food, and housing navigation stay connected while you study, so education doesn’t compete with survival. Shafanna Academy will host Shafanna Solutions’ Business Classes and homebuyer education, so learning connects directly to coaching and next steps.

Licensure & planned tracks: Shafanna Solutions is not yet an accredited or licensed school, proprietary vocational school, or FMCSA Training Provider Registry provider. Any future vocational, Tech Skills, or CDL driver-training tracks would be offered only through independent, qualified/registered training partners or after all required state, licensing, and FMCSA Entry-Level Driver Training (ELDT) approvals are secured.
